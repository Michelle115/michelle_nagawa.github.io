How it works

From first draft to publication

  1. Submit

    Entries come in through a school, a Kitabu Kids reading circle, or directly online — one story or poem per writer, per track.

  2. Mentor feedback

    Every entrant gets brief, encouraging written feedback from a volunteer writer-mentor — win or shortlist, everyone learns something.

  3. Shortlisting & judging

    A panel of Ugandan authors, teachers, and librarians reads shortlisted work blind (no names attached) and scores for voice, craft, and originality.

  4. Awards & public reading

    Winners are announced at a live awards event where shortlisted writers read their own work aloud to family, teachers, and past winners.

  5. Published in the anthology

    All shortlisted stories and poems are printed in the annual Kitabu Voices anthology, distributed to partner schools and libraries.

Why enter

Prizes & recognition

Publication

Every shortlisted piece appears in the printed anthology, with a copy for the writer and their school.

Mentorship

Category winners get a term of one-on-one mentoring with a published Ugandan writer.

Book vouchers

Winners and runners-up receive Kitabu Circles book vouchers and a one-year Familia subscription for their household.

Young Author of the Year

One overall winner across all age groups is named Kitabu Voices Young Author of the Year at the awards event.
