Design System · v1.0

Kitabo Circles

A brand and design language built for Ugandan reading communities — extracted and reimagined from Michelle Nagawa's academic portfolio, then redirected toward a warm, book-club identity for readers, schools, and young writers across Uganda.

Where this came from

Extracted from the source site

Original system

The existing portfolio (a Jekyll "minimal‑mistakes"‑style academic theme) uses a restrained, monochrome palette — #333332 body text, #111/#343434 accents, a single red highlight (#CC0000) — paired with PT Serif for body copy and PT Sans Narrow for headings on a fixed 16px/30px type rhythm. It reads as academic, quiet, and CV-first.

What carries over

We keep the same bones: a serif for long-form reading, a distinct sans for UI, a single accent color used sparingly, and a modular type scale. What changes is the palette and voice — trading grayscale-and-red for warm terracotta, banana-leaf green, and golden savanna tones, built for a community reading brand rather than a personal résumé.

Foundations

Color palette

Rooted in Uganda's landscape — red earth, banana leaves, Lake Victoria, and savanna gold — kept warm and print-like rather than literal or flag-coded.
