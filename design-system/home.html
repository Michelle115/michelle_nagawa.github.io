How it works

Three ways to belong

  1. Pick your circle

    Join an existing reading circle in your neighbourhood, workplace, or church/mosque group — or start your own with our starter kit and discussion guides.

  2. Read on your terms

    Physical books delivered monthly, or a low-data digital edition for readers on mobile. Every pick features at least one Ugandan or East African author.

  3. Meet, discuss, grow

    Guided discussion questions, in-person or WhatsApp-group meet-ups, and an annual Kitabu Circles Readers' Festival bring the community together in person.
