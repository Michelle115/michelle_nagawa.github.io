Why it matters

The reading gap starts early

Many Ugandan primary learners reach upper grades without ever having read a full storybook for pleasure. Kitabo Kids exists to close that gap — one classroom, one reading corner, one Reading Champion at a time.

How schools partner with us

From sign-up to a school-wide reading culture

  1. Sign up your school

    A short conversation with our schools team about class sizes, existing library resources, and language needs (English, Luganda, and other local languages welcome).

  2. Get your starter library kit

    Levelled storybooks in English and local languages, a classroom reading corner kit, and offline-friendly digital access for schools with shared tablets or a computer lab.

  3. Train a Reading Champion

    One teacher (or two) per school completes a short training on read-aloud technique, running a weekly reading circle, and using the Reading Passport with learners.

  4. Run weekly reading circles

    30-minute sessions where children read aloud, discuss, and — from age 8 up — start writing their own short pieces, feeding directly into Kitabo Voices.

  5. Celebrate at the Termly Reading Festival

    Each term ends with a school-wide showcase: reading passport badge ceremony, a favourite-book fair, and readings of children's own stories and poems.

Gamified reading

The Reading Passport

Every child gets a Reading Passport — a simple, physical or digital booklet that fills up with stamps and badges as they read. It turns finishing books into a journey kids want to keep going on.

First Chapter

Awarded for finishing your very first book in the program.

Bookworm

Ten books read — in any language, any genre.

Read-Aloud Star

Confidently read a story aloud to the class or a younger learner.

Storyteller

Wrote and shared an original short story or poem — the bridge into Kitabo Voices.

For teachers & parents

Support, not just books

Teacher toolkit

Ready-made lesson plans, read-aloud scripts, and simple ways to run a 30-minute reading circle without extra prep time.

Home reading guide

Short, translated tip sheets so parents and guardians can keep the reading habit going at home, even with just ten minutes a day.

Progress you can see

A simple termly report for teachers and head teachers showing books read, badges earned, and learners ready for the writing track.
